Frequently asked questions

Does Recolleat need an account or internet connection?

No account is needed. Saved entries work locally on this device. Initial installation and updates require internet access; your saved journal works offline.

How is a restaurant's overall score calculated?

Food contributes 60%, value 20% and service 20%. The Grand List follows this score; Ranking Laboratory lets you test alternative weights without changing your permanent rankings.

Where is my Palate Code, and why are some letters unknown?

Open My Profile from the Hub. Your four letters come from rated dishes and restaurant context. Small samples, limited price variation and near-neutral patterns leave a letter as ? instead of guessing. The maturity bar measures data coverage, not statistical certainty. The 16 possible personas are explained under World of Personas.

How do I record a visit without knowing the date?

Open the restaurant editor and add a visit with the date left blank. It counts as logged, but is excluded from month-specific charts.

Why do I see “10+” visits?

Restaurant visit badges show 10+ after ten visits. Recolleat retains the exact number internally. Your profile only counts actual logged visit records.

How do Quick Add and drafts work?

Tap Add, then Quick Add, to save the restaurant name, cuisine, location and optionally a food score. Drafts are private on this device and stay out of rankings until you finish the three-step editor. Drafts are included in new JSON backups.

How do I tell different branches apart?

Enter the precise branch name and neighbourhood. Each restaurant record remains independently editable.

How do I move my journal to another phone?

Export a JSON backup from the Hub, install Recolleat at the same website address on the new device, then import the backup. Changes do not synchronise automatically.

How do I install Recolleat on my phone?

On iPhone, open the website in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen. On other phones, open the site in Chrome and choose Add to Home screen or Install app. Open once while connected to the internet to initialise offline caching.

Where are my photos and restaurant details stored?

In browser storage on this device, not in a Recolleat cloud account. Clearing site data or removing the app can delete them, so export backups regularly.

Can I change the colours and switch dark mode?

Return to the Hub, then open Theme to choose a palette and light or dark mode.

How do I send feedback or report a bug?

Use the Send feedback link at the top of this page. Describe the screen and steps to reproduce; screenshots help.

RECOLLEAT / YOUR CONTROL

Privacy & Safety

Understand where your journal lives, what happens when you open an external service, and how to remove your local collection.

Stored on this device

Restaurant ratings, dishes, photos, visits and saved lists are stored in this browser's IndexedDB database. Your profile name, profile photo and appearance settings are stored in browser local storage. This edition does not provide accounts or automatic cloud synchronisation.

Clearing browser or app storage may erase the data. Export a backup before changing phones, browsers or website addresses.

Third-party websites

Recolleat does not query any external restaurant-search service in this edition.

To display the Fraunces typeface, the app requests a stylesheet and font from Google Fonts when online. Google can receive connection information associated with those requests. When offline or unable to load the font, Recolleat uses a serif fallback. Your hosting provider may separately process connection logs, cookies and other technical information. Review the respective providers’ privacy information.

Storage & cookies

The journal uses browser storage to remember your entries and settings. The application code does not set advertising, marketing or analytics cookies. No optional tracking consent is requested because no optional tracking technologies are loaded by this edition.

This is a description of Recolleat's application code, not a certification of the hosting provider's practices.

Your choices

You can edit entries, remove photographs, export your full journal, or erase the Recolleat data stored by this website in the current browser. Exported files or backups saved elsewhere are not automatically deleted.

There is no paid subscription, public profile, shared review feed or marketing email system in this edition.

Your data controls

Use the export control first if there is anything you may want to restore. Erasing device data cannot be undone from within the app.

Before a public commercial launch

The operator must publish an accurate, complete privacy notice with contact details, a reviewed legal basis and hosting disclosures, and suitable terms. Refund and cancellation information becomes relevant if payment features launch. Accounts, friends, public reviews, marketing and children’s access need separate compliance work before release.

This screen describes this version's operation; it is not a substitute for the operator's complete legal notices or a legal compliance audit.
